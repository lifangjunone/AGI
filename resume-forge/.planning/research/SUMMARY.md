# Research Summary

ResumeForge should compete on trustworthy job targeting rather than template quantity. Current resume guidance consistently favors a simple single-column document, standard section names, role-specific keywords, action verbs and quantified results. Market comparisons also expose recurring frustration with download paywalls and unclear resume-data handling.

## Product Implications

1. Use one semantic A4 template before adding visual variants.
2. Treat ATS score as an explainable checklist, never a hiring prediction.
3. Ask for evidence before generating polished language.
4. Keep local editing and HTML/PDF export free.
5. Monetize future multi-job versions, comparison and evidence-constrained AI assistance.
