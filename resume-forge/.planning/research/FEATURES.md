# Feature Research

## Table Stakes

- Structured personal, experience, education and skills editing.
- Live resume preview and PDF-compatible export.
- JD keyword comparison and ATS-readable single-column format.
- Action-led, quantified achievement bullets.
- Draft persistence and mobile editing.

## Differentiators

- Every rewrite is assembled from explicit user evidence.
- Match score exposes keyword and completeness components.
- Every bullet shows action, metric, impact and length checks.
- Resume data remains in the browser by default.

## Anti-Features

- No fake experience generation.
- No decorative multi-column template in v1.
- No forced account or download paywall.
- No auto-application workflow before resume quality is validated.
