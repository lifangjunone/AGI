# Opportunity Discovery Product Research

## Products Reviewed

- **Exploding Topics**: pairs a trend name with growth, volume, time window, category, and a short explanation.
- **Trend Hunter**: turns a signal into an editorial business implication with examples and visual context.
- **CB Insights**: structures companies, sectors, funding, maturity, and market maps for comparison.
- **G2**: anchors buying decisions in use case, rating, review volume, pricing, and integrations.
- **Product Hunt**: exposes launch timing, votes, comments, makers, categories, and direct user feedback.
- **Reddit pain discovery**: mines recurring first-person problems and validates them through comments and follow-up questions.

## Product Rules

An opportunity is not a trending topic. A useful opportunity record needs:

1. A specific buyer and costly problem.
2. A sellable first offer with a pricing hypothesis.
3. Independent evidence with source links.
4. A narrow seven-day demand test.
5. Explicit assumptions, risks, and falsification conditions.
6. A reason the user can execute now with available capabilities.

## Anti-Patterns

- Treating social engagement as willingness to pay.
- Repeating the same story from syndicated sources as independent confirmation.
- Recommending a broad market with no reachable buyer.
- Inventing market size or revenue projections not present in source data.
- Hiding regulation, platform dependency, or high acquisition cost.
- Ranking an opportunity highly because it contains popular AI terminology.

## Recommended Interface

Use a quiet macOS source-list layout with a daily opportunity queue, one selected opportunity in a detailed work area, and a visible validation pipeline. Lead with the offer and buyer, not an unexplained score. Keep evidence, risks, economics, and the seven-day experiment visible without forcing the user to infer what a metric means.
