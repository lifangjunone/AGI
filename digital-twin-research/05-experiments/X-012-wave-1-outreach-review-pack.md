## 锁定条款

最终三封邀请必须完全相同地披露：

- 这是 30 天、2×2、真实支付的随机实验，不是平台采购、免费 AI 克隆或长期合作承诺。
- 每名专家目标样本为 80 人；四组功能、价格、期限和提交额度相同。
- 一半参与者看见专家姓名，一半只看见匿名的授权知识代理。
- 一半参与者没有逐条真人复核，一半由同一位专家本人按统一 rubric 复核。
- 专家不能选择参与者、查看分配表或在实验结束前查看分组转化。
- 参与者由中立运营方从无研究报酬的商业流量招募；主样本不使用专家客户或粉丝名单，专家不得自行发帖、背书或发送邀请。
- 专家需要提供至少 30 个原创案例、20 份历史反馈和 50 个常见问答，并确认使用授权。
- 最坏复核责任为 40 名签认组参与者，每人最多 1 次本人签认，即最多 40 次复核；其余反馈不逐条复核。
- 所有组提供 7 天无条件退款；身份无增量、匿名组更好或实验失败都会如实记录。
- 下一步只是一场 30 分钟资格核验，不构成招募成功。

任何候选要求删除匿名组、改用免费意向、由本人渠道招募、改变组间价格、查看中期结果、由团队代替本人复核或压下负面结论，都必须记录为条件变更，不得口头接受。

## W01：Jessica Brody

收件人：Jessica Brody
垂直：小说与叙事写作
状态：未发送

```text
Hi Jessica,

I am preparing a 30-day randomized paid study to test whether customers value a specific expert's identity separately from that expert's method and human review. This is not a platform purchase, a free AI clone, or a request for a long-term commitment.

Your public work in story structure, plotting, character development, drafting, and revision makes you a possible fit for the writing arm. The proposed service would let each participant submit up to 2,000 words or one focused plot question per round. Feedback would be limited to structure, character motivation, scene goal, conflict, and the next revision step.

For one expert, the study targets 80 qualified participants randomly assigned to four equal-price groups:

1. your authorized identity, without per-response human review;
2. an anonymous authorized knowledge agent, without per-response human review;
3. your authorized identity, with your review under one fixed rubric; or
4. an anonymous authorized knowledge agent, with the same review by you but without displaying your name.

All groups receive the same 30-day access, functionality, price, response target, refund terms, and maximum of four submissions. Participants pay real money and receive a seven-day unconditional refund option.

An independent research party would recruit and assign participants through neutral commercial channels, without research compensation before the purchase outcome and without using your customer or fan lists. You would not select participants, send invitations, endorse the study publicly, see assignments, or see group conversion before the study ends.

Qualification would require permission to use at least 30 original examples, 20 pieces of historical feedback, and 50 frequently asked questions, plus explicit boundaries, escalation rules, and correction procedures. Each participant may make up to four submissions, but each human-review participant receives at most one review signed by you; the other submissions are not reviewed response by response. The worst-case load is therefore 40 short reviews over 30 days. The named and anonymous review groups must use the same rubric.

The study will preserve negative findings. If your displayed identity adds no value, if the anonymous condition performs better, or if the economics do not work, that result will be recorded as such.

The only next step is a 30-minute factual qualification call about paid transaction history, neutral audience recruitment without your customer or fan lists, content authorization, anonymity, review capacity, data handling, and publication of results. Interest or a call does not create a partnership or count as study recruitment.

Would you be open to that qualification call under these conditions?
```

- Writing Mastery Academy 学员不能构成主要样本，因为渠道会暴露身份。
- 多讲师、嘉宾、社群和团队内容不得进入知识快照或复核流程，只能使用 Jessica 本人的授权材料和本人复核。
- 当前公开状态为 `public_capacity_unproven / team_delivery_risk`。资格电话
  必须先完成逐项交付者/权利矩阵；会员访问权、Jessica 口头同意或 Writing
  Mastery 一方许可均不能自动覆盖其他讲师、学员、出版社或第三方材料。
- 必须用正式输入边界完成 5 份 Jessica 本人盲法计时校准，并证明第 75 百分位
  负担支持 40 次/30 天/24 小时目标时限；完成前不得运行候选级渠道预筛。
- 单次上限和反馈范围只在资格电话中缩小，不得扩大为完整手稿编辑。

## 不计数规则

- 表示兴趣或预约电话：只计为 `interested`，不计为通过。
- 完成资格电话：若证据不齐，只计为 `qualification_incomplete`。
